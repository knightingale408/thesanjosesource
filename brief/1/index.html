Brief #1 · Week of 2026-10-12

City Council’s October 20 agenda lists the 2025 Independent Police Auditor Year End Report, and the earlier high-impact items in this pull have no posted vote.

By J Gonzales · Subjects: San José City Council; budget; housing; public safety; fees; contracts

This week’s brief

  • No votes are posted for any item from September 15 through October 6. Source: Legistar calendar
  • No dollar figures are posted for any item in this pull.
  • The October 20 Independent Police Auditor year-end report is the only upcoming item. Source: 26-1078
  • Arena Hotel and Homekey actions have no posted outcome. Source: 26-1035
  • The Measure T status report was renumbered from 26-1002 to 26-1045. Neither row has a posted tally. Source: 26-1045

Why it matters

The October 20 agenda carries one item from this pull: the 2025 Independent Police Auditor Year End Report. Legistar files it under Public Safety, and the status is Agenda Ready. The record for that item does not include a vote, a dollar figure, or a stated next step. Source: 26-1078

The other items are already on agendas from September 15 through October 6. They cover housing, fees, and budget reports, including the Arena Hotel and Homekey actions and affordable housing at 1510 North 1st Street. This pull does not include a posted vote or a dollar figure for any of them either. Source: 26-1035

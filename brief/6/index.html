Each item below is one sentence from its file page, the staff recommendation. The link goes to that page.

  • File 26-1186. (a) Adopt a resolution authorizing the City Manager or her designee to: (1) Negotiate and execute an amendment to the Agreement for a Customer Information System Infinity Utility Billing System Upgrade with N. Harris Computer Corporation (Ottawa, ON, Canada) to extend the initial two-year term to three years, through on or about November 30, 2027, or as may be adjusted to align with the software subscription term, and increase the maximum compensation not to exceed $2,227,950, subject to the appropriation of funds; and
  • File 26-1188. Adopt a resolution authorizing the City Manager or her designee to: (a) Amend purchase order OP 67528 with Owen Equipment Sales (Fairfield, California), for the purchase of as required sewage cleaning machine rental services, to increase the maximum compensation by up to $400,000, for a revised maximum compensation not to exceed $920,000, for the period ending November 30, 2026, subject to the appropriation of funds; and
  • File 26-1192. (a) Adopt a resolution authorizing the Housing Director, or his designee, to negotiate and execute the Third Amendment to the Standard Consultant Agreement with CSG Advisors Incorporated to increase compensation by $1,000,000, for a total maximum compensation not to exceed $2,000,000, for housing finance and development advisory and housing policy consulting services, for the term ending June 30, 2027.
  • File 26-1196. Approve the Master Consultant Agreement with Black & Veatch Corporation to provide Owner’s Advisor services for the 10668 – Pump Stations Improvements Phase 1 Project at the San José-Santa Clara Regional Wastewater Facility from the date of execution through December 31, 2036, in a total amount not to exceed $16,885,000, subject to the appropriation of funds.
  • File 26-1197. (a) Conduct a public hearing on the applications of the 32 companies listed below for nonexclusive franchises for the collection, transport, and delivery of residential clean-out material and construction and demolition debris.
  • File 26-1187. Adopt a resolution authorizing the City Manager or her designee to: (a) Execute a purchase order with Bear Electrical Solutions, LLC (Alviso, California), for the purchase of fiber optic cable services beginning on or about November 17, 2026, and ending on or about November 16, 2027, for a not to exceed amount of $700,000, subject to the appropriation of funds;
  • File 26-1189. Adopt a resolution authorizing the City Manager or her designee to amend purchase order OP 68069 with SNF Polydyne Inc. (Riceboro, Georgia), for the purchase of polymers for co-thickening and centrifuge dewatering to increase the maximum compensation by up to $1,950,000, for a revised maximum compensation not to exceed $4,700,000 for the period ending June 30, 2027, subject to the appropriation of funds.
  • File 26-1190. Adopt a resolution authorizing the City Manager or her designee to amend purchase order OP 65438 with Technology Engineering & Construction, Inc. dba TEC Acutite (San Francisco, California), for fuel storage tank and dispensing equipment maintenance, repair, inspection, and testing services to increase the maximum compensation by up to $800,000, for a revised maximum compensation not to exceed $1,200,000, for the period ending March 31, 2027, subject to the appropriation of funds.

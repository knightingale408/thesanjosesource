What the staff memo recommends

From the staff memorandum, Erik L. Soliván, Jim Shannon. Staff memorandum (PDF).

(a) Adopt a resolution authorizing the Housing Director, or his designee, to negotiate and execute the Third Amendment to the Standard Consultant Agreement with CSG Advisors Incorporated to increase compensation by $1,000,000, for a total maximum compensation not to exceed $2,000,000, for housing finance and development advisory and housing policy consulting services, for the term ending June 30, 2027.

(b) Adopt the following 2026-2027 Appropriation Ordinance amendments in the Low and Moderate Income Housing Asset Fund:

(1) Decrease the Unrestricted Ending Fund Balance by $1,000,000; and

(2) Increase the Non-Personal/Equipment appropriation to the Housing Department by $1,000,000.

What changes for a resident

The memorandum’s fiscal impacts section says:

The actions recommended in this memorandum increase the maximum compensation with CSG Advisors by $1,000,000 to support the expanded scope of work, which includes expanded financial review of distressed developments within the City’s affordable housing portfolio, development of policies and procedures in response to the recent City audit of the Department’s loan portfolio, ongoing feasibility analyses of sites across San José, and review of housing development proposals. The additional $1,000,000 will be funded through the Low and Moderate Income Housing Asset Fund. The costs will be incurred on an as-needed basis through individual service orders issued during the term of the agreement, which runs through June 30, 2027.

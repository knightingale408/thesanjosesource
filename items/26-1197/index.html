What changes for a resident

The memorandum’s fiscal impacts section says:

The non-exclusive franchise agreements have not been a significant source of revenue.

For Fiscal Year 2025-2026, the amount collected from non-exclusive haulers was $53,174.16. The Franchise Fee is set at $11.01 per cubic yard, or fraction thereof, of compacted solid waste, and $3.67 per cubic yard, or fraction thereof, of uncompacted solid waste, and the AB 939 Fee is $13 per debris box, per haul. Revenue from these fees cannot be estimated, making this revenue stream unpredictable.

All applicants have paid the application fee of $1,326.00, as required by San José Municipal Code Section 9.10.1670.

What changes for a resident

The memorandum’s fiscal impacts section says:

The recommended amendment to the agreement requires increases to the Environmental Services Department Non-Personal/Equipment appropriations in each of the following funds: the Water Utility Operating Fund ($386,901), Integrated Waste Management Fund ($193,449), Sewer Service and Use Charge Fund ($134,573), and Storm and Sewer Operating Fund ($126,162). This amendment would add one year to the initial term and increase the not-to-exceed contract amount by $841,085, from approximately $1.4 million to $2.2 million, while allowing further change orders and amendments subject to the appropriation of funds.

Current subscription, maintenance, and support costs for version 3 of the CIS software are $97,382. The ongoing subscription, maintenance, and support costs for the version 5 upgrade are estimated to total $223,904 per year after the upgrade. Additional ongoing costs of $89,750 will be incurred if the City elects to pursue additional optional features to further enhance the CIS, with the maximum ongoing costs totaling $313,654.

The 2026-2027 Operating Budget currently contains $1.4 million for the upgrade and $97,382 for the ongoing costs. Any additional ongoing costs will be included in the development of the 2027-2028 Operating Budget.

Adopt a resolution authorizing “Free Use” of the Janet Gray Hayes Rotunda, Council Chambers, Wing Conference Rooms, City Hall garage, and Fourth Street garage for the private Youth Empowerment Summit to be held on Saturday, April 17, 2027, co-hosted by the Library and Parks, Recreation and Neighborhood Services Departments.

What changes for a resident

The memorandum’s fiscal impacts section says:

The Library Department will provide onsite staff to facilitate and oversee this event in partnership with the Public Works Department, Event Services Division and PRNS. The total costs associated with the event, including but not limited to staffing, security, audio/visual personnel, cleaning fee, pre-planning, and post-event, etc., are $14,313.

After accounting for City Council approval for “Free Use”, only $5,290 will be necessary for staff, security, and the application fee, which will be paid from the Library Department’s Non-Personal/Equipment appropriation in the General Fund with funding designated for the Youth Commission Summit by the City Council.

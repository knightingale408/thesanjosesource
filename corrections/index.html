Corrections

Every change to a published page, in one place

By J Gonzales · Updated November 19, 2026

When we get something wrong, we say so, date it and keep the original wording. This page lists every correction, update and clarification, newest first. Each entry links to the page, where the full record and the original wording are kept.

Change log

  • Update Nov. 19, 2026 · Promised vs. Actual tracker

    A date, budget, status or history entry stays off its project card until that record passes the source check. Previous wording, from the November 11 update: “Values that have not finished our source check are labeled ‘Verification pending.’” The cards now say “Awaiting source check” instead of showing those values. The line above the headline changed from “Promised vs. Actual · Updated November 11, 2026” to “Promised vs. Actual · Updated November 19, 2026.” No date, figure or source in the tracked records was changed, and none was marked accepted.

  • Update Nov. 18, 2026 · All briefs

    Replaced brief numbers with short labels that say when each brief ran. “Brief #1 · Council, Sept. 15–Oct. 6” is now “Decided Sept. 15–Oct. 6.” “Brief #2 · Promised vs. Actual” is now “Promised vs. Actual.” “Brief #3 · Council, Nov. 17” is now “Before the Nov. 17 vote.” Publish, correction and update dates moved from the label to the byline line under the headline. Page addresses, headlines, facts, figures and sources did not change.

  • Correction and update Nov. 17, 2026 · Wastewater contracts and data center pause

    Corrected the remaining pump-station votes from “twice more” to “three more times,” and the late 2028 schedule line from “a design-build contract” to “the design-builder's contract for preliminary services.” Added the environmental findings in the second half of 2028. Updated the minutes line: the Oct. 27 minutes were approved 11-0 on Nov. 10; the Oct. 20 results are still from draft minutes. The original wording is kept on the brief page.

  • Correction Nov. 16, 2026 · Wastewater contracts and data center pause

    Changed the headline, subtitle and two sentences to match the City staff memos. The headline said “Staff cite data center boom”; the memo says data center development “may have” contributed to higher bids. The subtitle said a building surge “left only two contractors bidding”; the memo says one of three pre-qualified contractors declined to bid. A line said the adviser contract has “no impact” on the General Fund; the memo says “no additional impact.” The original wording is kept on the brief page.

  • Update Nov. 11, 2026 · Promised vs. Actual tracker

    Replaced the Projects table with scorecards generated from the site's tracked data (public-data/projects.json): promised vs. latest reported, project stage, change history and the evidence behind each value. Added category, status and funding-measure filters with shareable links, and a "Verification pending" label on values that have not finished our source check. No dates, figures or sources changed.

  • Update Nov. 7, 2026 · Fee waiver, housing loan and police overtime and Fire stations and police training center

    Labeled our briefs “Brief #1,” “Brief #2” and so on, in the order they were published, to match Substack. Labels changed from “Weekly brief · No. 1” and “Promised vs. Actual · Brief No. 2.” Brief #2’s browser-tab and link-preview title changed from “Brief #2 — The San Jose Source” to its headline. Page addresses, facts, figures and wording did not change.

  • Update Oct. 18, 2026 · Fire stations and police training center

    Reversed the Oct. 17 link change. The original Annual Report links were correct; the replacements did not open. No facts, figures or wording changed.

  • Update and clarification Oct. 18, 2026 · Promised vs. Actual tracker

    Added Fire Station 36 and the section “Built is not the same as open.” Clarified what “Promised” means, because the earlier definition was inaccurate. No existing dates, figures or sources changed.

  • Update Oct. 17, 2026 · Fee waiver, housing loan and police overtime

    Reorganized the page, rewrote it in plain language and changed the headline. No facts, figures or sources changed.

  • Update Oct. 17, 2026 · Fire stations and police training center

    Changed the headline, added a line explaining the Promised vs. Actual series, and replaced three Annual Report links (reversed Oct. 18).

  • Update Oct. 16, 2026 · Fee waiver, housing loan and police overtime

    Withdrew Jay’s take because its premise was corrected Oct. 14; the original text is kept in full on the page. Marked the Oct. 15 committee meeting as held, outcome not yet checked.

  • Correction Oct. 14, 2026 · Fee waiver, housing loan and police overtime

    The brief said no votes or dollar figures were posted for Council items from Sept. 15 to Oct. 6. That was wrong: the City Clerk’s draft minutes record every outcome, and two actions carry dollar figures. We corrected the affected lines and added the outcomes.

  • Update Oct. 13, 2026 · Fee waiver, housing loan and police overtime

    Added a section on police recruiting overtime and corrected two summary lines the new section made inaccurate. Disclosed a figure choice: the Police Department report gives 119 departures on one page and 120 on another; we use 119.

What the labels mean

  • Correction: something we published was wrong. The correction also goes at the top of that page.
  • Update: new information was added, or a headline or presentation changed.
  • Clarification: the facts were right, but the wording could mislead.

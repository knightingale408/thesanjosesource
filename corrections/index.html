    The earlier November 19 update is kept below. It said: “The cards now say ‘Awaiting source check’ instead of showing those values.” It also said: “No date, figure or source in the tracked records was changed, and none was marked accepted.” Five records are now accepted, and those values show on the cards. Police Training and Academy Facility: the end-of-2023 promise (file 21-1392, page 6, “expected to be completed at the end of 2023”) and the September 29, 2026 update (beneficial use in December 2026; file 26-1045, page 4). New Fire Station 32: the September 29, 2026 update only (beneficial use in September 2026; file 26-1045, page 3). The original promise for Fire Station 32 is still awaiting source check. New Fire Station 36, Coyote Creek Flood Protection Project (Phase 2), and Pure Water Silicon Valley still say “Awaiting source check.” Dollar figures that are still awaiting source check are off this page. Fire Station 36 previously said: “still in design. City staff estimate $4.5 million in added site work. On Oct. 20 the Council approved $1.0 million of it, raising the project budget from $15.5 million to $16.5 million; staff expect to bring the remaining $3.5 million in the next capital budget or with the construction contract.” The dollar sentences are removed. The line now says the station is still in design, and cites the September 8, 2026 status report schedule: “Project currently in design” (file 26-1002, Attachment B; the same memorandum is file 26-1045). The section “The one dollar figure” previously said: “Pure Water stores $3,295,950 as both the promised and actual amount. That is the grant total already cited ($1,092,000 and $2,203,950). The linked report does not give a construction cost difference, so the variance cell stays not stated.” That section is removed. Fire Station 32’s card and the paragraph under the cards name different milestones. Previous wording: “the Fire Department says it has reached substantial completion and expects the temporary occupancy certificate in October 2026.” The card’s September 2026 date is the status report’s beneficial-use date (file 26-1045, page 3, and file 26-1002, page 3): the project is expected to achieve beneficial use in September 2026. The Fire Department memo (September 24, 2026, page 20) says the station has reached substantial completion, with punch list items and the vehicular gate remaining, and that temporary certification of occupancy is expected in October 2026. The paragraph now states both.

What the labels mean

  • Correction: something we published was wrong. The correction also goes at the top of that page.
  • Update: new information was added, or a headline or presentation changed.
  • Clarification: the facts were right, but the wording could mislead.

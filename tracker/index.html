Promised vs. Actual · Updated November 11, 2026

Six San José projects: the City’s first target dates, and what happened

By J Gonzales

We only calculate a delay or a budget difference when the City documents give exact dates or amounts. Where they give only a month, a phrase such as “about two months,” or the same grant total for both the plan and the result, the card shows not stated, meaning the linked documents do not give that number.

Promised is the earliest completion estimate we found in a City staff report. For the Measure T fire stations and police training center, an earlier City budget document (the 2019-20 Budget in Brief) listed a target fiscal year; those are in our Measure T brief. As an exception, Fire Station 36’s row uses that earlier budget-document target for Promised and shows the staff estimates under Revised. All of these are the City’s own planning targets, not contractual promises. Revised is the latest target before completion. The full history for the fire stations and police training center is in our Measure T brief.

Built is not the same as open

A finished building and a staffed, operating station are separate milestones. The table tracks construction; this is where each project stands on opening, in the City’s words.

  • Fire Station 8: occupancy certificate July 21, 2026; the crew began operating there July 22. Source: 26-1002
  • Fire Station 32: the Fire Department says it has reached substantial completion and expects the temporary occupancy certificate in October 2026. The 2026-27 budget deferred activating the station for two years, to 2028-29. On Oct. 20 the Council approved the City Manager’s Annual Report, which funds a partial activation beginning Oct. 1, 2026: the station temporarily houses the Community Paramedicine pilot program and is occupied for building security and to test its new systems. Full activation stays set for 2028-29, or earlier if the City’s budget improves. Sources: Fire Dept. memo, 26-1002, 26-1076 (Annual Report memo p. 19; Section IV pp. 117, 178), Oct. 20 draft minutes
  • Police Training and Academy Facility: expected ready for City use (beneficial use) in December 2026. Activation was also deferred to 2028-29. The Annual Report the Council approved Oct. 20 funds a partial activation beginning Jan. 1, 2027, when the Police Training Unit moves in from the South San José Police Substation. Sources: 26-1002, 26-1076 (Annual Report memo p. 19; Section IV pp. 117, 178), Oct. 20 draft minutes
  • Fire Station 36: still in design. City staff estimate $4.5 million in added site work. On Oct. 20 the Council approved $1.0 million of it, raising the project budget from $15.5 million to $16.5 million; staff expect to bring the remaining $3.5 million in the next capital budget or with the construction contract. Sources: 26-1076 (Annual Report memo p. 19; Section IV pp. 117, 178), Oct. 20 draft minutes

The one dollar figure

Pure Water stores $3,295,950 as both the promised and actual amount. That is the grant total already cited ($1,092,000 and $2,203,950). The linked report does not give a construction cost difference, so the variance cell stays not stated. Source: 26-1100.

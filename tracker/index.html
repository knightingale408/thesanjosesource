Six San José projects: the City’s first target dates, and what happened

By J Gonzales

We only calculate a delay or a budget difference when the City documents give exact dates or amounts. Where they give only a month, a phrase such as “about two months,” or the same grant total for both the plan and the result, the cell says not stated, meaning the linked documents do not give that number.

Promised is the earliest completion estimate we found in a City staff report. For the Measure T fire stations and police training center, an earlier City budget document (the 2019-20 Budget in Brief) listed a target fiscal year; those are in our Measure T brief. As an exception, Fire Station 36’s row uses that earlier budget-document target for Promised and shows the staff estimates under Revised. All of these are the City’s own planning targets, not contractual promises. Revised is the latest target before completion. Built is not the same as open

A finished building and a staffed, operating station are separate milestones. The table tracks construction; this is where each project stands on opening, in the City’s words.

  • Fire Station 8: occupancy certificate July 21, 2026; the crew began operating there July 22. Source: 26-1002
  • Fire Station 32: the Fire Department says it has reached substantial completion and expects the temporary occupancy certificate in October 2026. The 2026-27 budget deferred activating the station for two years, to 2028-29. The City Manager’s Annual Report on the Oct. 20 Council agenda proposes a partial activation in October 2026 for building security and to run the new systems. Sources: Fire Dept. memo, 26-1002, 26-1076
  • Police Training and Academy Facility: expected ready for City use (beneficial use) in December 2026. Activation was also deferred to 2028-29; the Annual Report proposes a partial activation in January 2027. Sources: 26-1002, 26-1076
  • Fire Station 36: still in design. City staff estimate $4.5 million in added site work; the Annual Report recommends $1.0 million now. Council has not acted on it. Source: 26-1076

The one dollar figure

Pure Water stores $3,295,950 as both the promised and actual amount. That is the grant total already cited ($1,092,000 and $2,203,950). The linked report does not give a construction cost difference, so the variance cell stays not stated. Source: 26-1100.

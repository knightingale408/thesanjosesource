fiscal: "Pursuant to the Schedule of Fees Resolution No. 79138, City departments may exercise a “Free Use” option for qualifying events up to five times each calendar year.\n\nApproval of the action associated with this memorandum will count towards one of the Department of Planning, Building and Code Enforcement “Free Use” options. The approval of a Saturday “Free Use” will apply to one of the five “Free Use” times in the calendar year. The estimated total cost for the reservation is $6,708.60, of which $5,470.46 would be waived because of the approval of this “Free Use” request. The remaining $1,075.64 in fees and $162.50 in Event Services administrative fees will be paid by the City Manager’s Office of Economic Development and Cultural Affairs from the Cultural Grants and Programs appropriation in the Transient Occupancy Tax Fund for the application fee, staffing, and security costs associated with the use of City Hall East and West Plazas."
